How it works

From a CT scan to an estimate in four steps

01

Scan

A chest CT is a stack of hundreds of X-ray slices that together form a 3D image of the lungs.

02

Focus

A 48 mm cube around the nodule you select is cut out and resampled to 1 mm voxels.

03

Analyse

Five 3D convolutional neural networks each study the nodule's shape, edges and density.

04

Explain

Their votes are averaged, and a heatmap shows which regions drove the decision.
